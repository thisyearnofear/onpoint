## The job

One clip for the Photon "Muse" X post that makes a stranger want to text a
phone number. It must communicate two things at once:

1. **The surface** — Runway: an AI fashion judge in your iMessage. Text
   "play", get a theme, get 3 minutes, send a closet photo, get scored and
   roasted, get "the fix" — the piece that would have won.
2. **The product underneath** — the fix is not a stock photo. It's a real
   listing on OnPoint, the agentic fashion rail where you can try things on
   and agents can actually buy. The game is the fun front door; the platform
   is real.

Category pitch: **Most Unhinged** (primary), Most Inspiring (secondary).

## Concept: "The text that sends you sprinting to your closet"

The viral mechanic already proven by Dress to Impress IRL: *a digital game's
rules imposed on a real body*. Our version: **a text message gives a real
human 3 minutes to dress for a theme, and a savage AI judge scores the
result.** The comedy is physical (the closet raid) plus verbal (the verdict).

### Alt concept (backup): "Group chat runway"

Two friends, one group chat with the judge, each sends a photo, judge scores
both — built-in conflict and a winner. Stronger social spread but Phase B
(group + poll) isn't live yet, so only use this if the primary concept
underdelivers in review. Don't fake group features that don't exist.

## Production notes

- **Record the real line.** The judge must actually answer — no fake
  screenshots. Today's engine works end-to-end on prod; if a theme is weak
  for comedy, "again" deals another. Best themes for visual comedy:
  *Villain Era, Brat Summer, Wedding Guest (Not the Bride), Clash on
  Purpose, Red Carpet from Your Closet*.
- **Judge pick: Edina** for the clip (caps-lock chaos reads best on camera).
  Miranda as alt — icy burns contrast well against the panic.
- **Judging takes ~30–60s** on the free-tier model. That's the suspense
  beat, not dead air — cut on the typing dots, don't tighten it to zero.
- **Format:** 9:16 primary (phone-native), captions burned in for sound-off
  viewing, ≤60s. Deliver a second crop only if cheap.
- **Chat-history screenshot** for the Typeform: capture the full round in
  the thread — intro → theme → photo → verdict → card → fix link. Crop/mask
  your personal number; the judge line stays visible.
- **Own photo only.** Don't submit anyone else's closet pic. The game never
  stores photos (design claim) — keep that true on camera.
- **Don't promise what isn't real:** no group mode, no polls, no
  "agents bought my outfit" — agent checkout exists but isn't the clip's
  story. The fix tap-through to a live look page is the honest product beat.
- **On the verdict:** the judge roasts outfits, never bodies. If a take
  reads as mocking the person, reshoot the beat — that line is a guardrail,
  not a style choice.

## What makes this the product video, not just a game clip

One explicit beat (the fix → look page) carries the payload: *the judge
isn't a toy — it's a surface over a real fashion platform where the
recommended piece is buyable, try-on-able, and agent-accessible.* If a
viewer only remembers the game, we win Unhinged; if they clock that the fix
is real inventory, we win the product story.
